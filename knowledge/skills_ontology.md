# Skills ontology and domain glossary

Skills cluster. Matching a brief skill against a profile should consider the whole cluster, with exact matches weighted highest.

## Engineering clusters

- **Backend languages**: Go (Golang), Java, Kotlin, Python, Node.js, C#, Rust, Scala, Ruby, PHP.
- **Data stores**: PostgreSQL (Postgres), MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, Cassandra, DynamoDB, Elasticsearch, ClickHouse.
- **Messaging / streaming**: Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS, Pub/Sub, Kinesis.
- **Distributed systems**: microservices, gRPC, event-driven architecture, CQRS, idempotency, consensus, sharding, consistency, queues.
- **Cloud / infra**: AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes (K8s), Docker, Terraform, Helm, CI/CD, observability (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).
- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Next.js, Vue, Angular, CSS, Webpack.
- **Mobile**: Android (Kotlin), iOS (Swift), Flutter, React Native.
- **Data/ML**: Spark, Airflow, dbt, Snowflake, BigQuery, PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, MLOps, NLP, LLMs.
- **Embedded**: C, C++, Rust, RTOS, firmware, device drivers, I2C/SPI, satellite and avionics software.
- **Security**: AppSec, threat modelling, SAST/DAST, IAM, secrets management, PCI-DSS.

## Payments and fintech glossary (strong domain signals when they appear in ROLE descriptions)

payments, payment gateway, PSP (payment service provider), acquiring, issuing, card issuing, UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, NACH,
BBPS, wallets, PPI, ledger, double-entry ledger, reconciliation, settlements, payouts, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, fraud detection,
risk engine, KYC, AML, tokenisation, PCI-DSS, ISO 8583, 3-D Secure, merchant onboarding, lending, loan origination, BNPL,
core banking, open banking, account aggregator.

## Product / growth / sales vocabulary

- **Product**: roadmap, PRD, discovery, A/B testing, experimentation, OKRs, funnel, activation, retention, pricing, 0-to-1.
- **Growth marketing**: SEO, SEM, paid social, lifecycle, CRM, referral loops, attribution, CAC, LTV, cohort analysis.
- **Sales**: outbound, enterprise sales, quota, pipeline, MEDDIC, solution selling, SaaS, renewals, upsell.
- **Design**: Figma, design systems, prototyping, usability testing, interaction design.

## Company stage vocabulary

- **Startup / early stage**: seed, Series A, founding team, "wore many hats", 0-to-1, small team, ownership.
- **Scale-up**: Series B-D, hypergrowth, scaling teams, platformisation.
- **Enterprise**: large org, compliance, process, multi-team coordination.
"Shipped payments systems at a startup" therefore needs both a payments-domain signal and a startup-stage signal in dated roles.
